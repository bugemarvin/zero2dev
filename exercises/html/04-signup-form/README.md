# A sign-up form

Build a form in `index.html`.

- The form sends its data with `method="post"` to `action="/signup"`.
- A text field with `id` and `name` equal to `username`. It is required and has `minlength="3"`.
- An email field with `id` and `name` equal to `email`. It is required.
- A password field with `id` and `name` equal to `password`. It is required and has `minlength="8"`.
- A `select` with `id` and `name` equal to `plan`, with three options whose values are `free`, `pro` and `team`.
- A checkbox with `id` and `name` equal to `terms`. It is required.
- **Each of the five fields has a `label`** whose `for` matches the field's `id`.
- A submit button with the text `Create account`.
