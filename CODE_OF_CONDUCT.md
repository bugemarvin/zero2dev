# Code of conduct

zero2dev exists so that anyone can learn to program, starting from nothing. The project is run in the same spirit.

## What we expect

- **Be kind.** Many people here are beginners. A question that seems obvious to you was hard for you once.
- **Be patient with language.** English is a second or third language for many contributors. Answer what was meant.
- **Criticise the work, not the person.** "This exercise accepts a wrong answer" helps. "Who wrote this?" does not.
- **Assume good intent, and explain your own.** Say why you suggest a change.
- **Give credit.** Thank people. Name the source of ideas and material.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, mockery or contempt, including about someone's skill level, questions or English.
- Harassment of any kind: unwelcome attention, intimidation, following someone across platforms.
- Discrimination or demeaning remarks about gender, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, race, ethnicity, nationality, age or religion.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sexual language or imagery.

This applies everywhere the project lives: issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and the lessons and jokes themselves. A joke in the guide must be one that every learner can laugh at.

## If something goes wrong

Tell the maintainer, [@bugemarvin](https://github.com/bugemarvin), privately through GitHub. Say what happened and where. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and other contributions that break these rules, and may block a person who keeps breaking them, temporarily or for good.
